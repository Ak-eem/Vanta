# The new screen (branch ui-redo)

- **Look:** one palette in two versions. Light (warm paper, dark ink, deep blue) from 07:00 to 18:59, dark otherwise. Sidebar -> Auto / Light / Dark overrides it. The orb draws solid and dark on light.
- **Sidebar:** Voice/Chat switch, Home, This computer, Settings, recent presentations, look switch, privacy + pause. Folds away after 6 quiet seconds. A small button brings it back; it fades to a dot, moving the mouse to the left edge (or swiping from it on a phone) shows it again.
- **Voice mode:** the orb is alone in the middle. When VANTA presents something the orb glides to the bottom-right and the presentation fills the screen. Mic, mute, stop-talking and type-instead controls. VANTA reads replies aloud sentence by sentence while they are still being written (browser voice for now; swappable).
- **The table:** VANTA says how big each thing should be (`size`: s, m, l, full on a block; otherwise the type decides) and the layout packs them on a 12-column table. Linked diagrams and research reports keep their own layouts.
- **Chat mode:** works (conversation + presentation beside it), but is the plain version. Fuller chat comes next.
- Not built yet: wake word, emotional voice, sound effects, dragging/resizing panels, narration lines written by the model while research runs.
