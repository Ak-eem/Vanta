# The new screen (branch ui-redo)

- **Look:** one palette in two versions. Light (warm paper, dark ink, deep blue) from 07:00 to 18:59, dark otherwise. Sidebar -> Auto / Light / Dark overrides it. The orb draws solid and dark on light.
- **Sidebar:** Voice/Chat switch, Home, This computer, Settings, recent presentations, look switch, privacy + pause. Folds away after 6 quiet seconds. A small button brings it back; it fades to a dot, moving the mouse to the left edge (or swiping from it on a phone) shows it again.
- **Voice mode:** the orb is alone in the middle. When VANTA presents something the orb glides to the bottom-right and the presentation fills the screen. Mic, mute, stop-talking and type-instead controls. VANTA reads replies aloud sentence by sentence while they are still being written (browser voice for now; swappable).
- **The table:** VANTA says how big each thing should be (`size`: s, m, l, full on a block; otherwise the type decides) and the layout packs them on a 12-column table. Linked diagrams and research reports keep their own layouts.
- **Chat mode:** a calm single column: greeting with four starter ideas, you as a soft pill, VANTA as plain text, progress notes as quiet grey lines, presentations as a tappable card, a floating input, and the orb as a small badge on top. A presentation opens beside the chat on wide screens.
- **Live narration:** while a research report runs, VANTA says short lines in its own words (written by the fast model from verified information only: counts, site names, checked claims). At most 7 lines, at least 5 seconds apart, plus a closing line from the report's own summary. In voice mode they are spoken; in chat mode they are the grey notes. If the model is down, it stays silent instead of guessing. Code: `research/narrate.py`.
- **This computer:** the system dashboard, restyled to the same look.
- **Voice details:** Space (or the mic button) starts and stops listening. "Hey VANTA" toggle (ear button) listens for the name with the browser's speech recognition: Chrome/Edge only, needs internet, off by default, and a visible "Listening for Hey VANTA" note shows while it's on. VANTA picks the most natural browser voice it can find (you can choose another in the sidebar). In voice mode the model is told its reply will be spoken and may start a sentence with a delivery cue: (urgent) (excited) (gentle) (dry) (sighs) (laughs). The browser voice can only act these out with speed, pitch and pauses; `(laughs)` says "Ha ha." Cues are hidden on screen. Code: `lib/cues.ts`, `lib/wake.ts`, `vanta_ui/voicestyle.py`. The orb pulses with each spoken word and with your mic level.
- Not built yet: a truly emotional voice (needs a better speech engine), sound effects, dragging/resizing panels, narration for tasks other than research reports.
