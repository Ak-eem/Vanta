---
name: website-build
description: Use when building a website for a client (small business, portfolio, landing page).
---
1. Ask or infer: who the customer is, the one action a visitor should take (book, call, buy), and the brand feel.
2. Plan sections before code: hero with the main action, proof (reviews or photos), services/prices, contact/map, footer.
3. Mobile first. Most Nigerian visitors are on phones. One column, thumb-sized buttons, readable at 16px+.
4. One font pair, one accent colour, lots of spacing. Real copy, never lorem ipsum.
5. Keep it light: compress images, no heavy libraries, motion only where it guides the eye.
6. Check before finishing: every link and button works, form has a fallback (WhatsApp/phone), titles and descriptions set, page loads fast, no text under 14px.
Pitfalls: huge hero videos, ten fonts, buttons that don't say what they do, forgetting the phone number.
